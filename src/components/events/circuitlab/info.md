# Circuit Lab C — 2027 Rules Summary

**Status:** Verified against the supplied Division C Rules Manual, pp. C16–C17 (PDF pp. 21–22), checked October 8, 2026.

## Format and resources
- Team of up to 2; approximately 50 minutes; Class III calculators (up to two per team).
- Paper notes/resources may be any size/content/source; binders, books, removable pages, tabs and labels are allowed. ES supplies hands-on materials and measurement devices. Teams may bring basic multimeters at the ES’s discretion.
- At least one hands-on task. Participants must know breadboards and multimeters; they may ask about breadboard internal wiring. Hazardous soldering, high-power and AC-circuit exposure are prohibited.

## Written-test scope
At least one question from each listed topic: historical figures Ampere, Coulomb, Kirchhoff, Volta, Ohm, Hertz and Boole; charge/fields and Coulomb’s law; DC/AC characteristics and hazards; current, voltage, resistance, power and energy; DC circuits/components, series/parallel, open/short, Ohm’s law and schematics; silicon PN-junction devices including diodes/LEDs; Kirchhoff’s laws; digital logic, Boolean expressions, De Morgan’s law and combinational circuits.
- State/National additions: clock/timing concepts and flip-flops; PNP/NPN bias/gain and simple transistor logic; ideal op-amp principles/applications.
- Explicit exclusions: semiconductor topics beyond listed devices; AC circuit theory/frequency analysis; capacitance; inductance; unlisted nonlinear devices; three-phase power; Thevenin/Norton equivalents; oscilloscopes. Math excludes complex numbers, matrices and calculus.

## Hands-on and scoring
Possible tasks include voltage-only mystery-resistor measurement, power, equally bright LEDs, USB charger analysis, logic circuit construction, troubleshooting, 3-way switches and equivalent resistance. High score wins; written work is 50–75%, hands-on is the remaining 25–50%; preselected questions break ties.
