# Boomilever C — verified 2027 tool plan

Source: supplied 2027 Division C Rules Manual, pp. C7–C12. Prior checks using a 5,000 g bonus or earlier contact geometry are invalid.

## Structure compliance worksheet
Let teams record material/adhesive, wall-contact lines, chain position, and pre-load loading-block height. Check only explicit constraints: wood bonded by adhesive; chain 40–45 cm horizontally from wall and within 2.5 cm of centerline; contact outside vertical width lines; loading block above bolt centerline before sand; base contacts above 15 cm or bonus contacts above 10 cm. Report each rule separately and cite its section; do not claim to pre-judge an ES inspection.

## Load efficiency and bonus estimator
Use `Load Scored = Load Supported + 7,500 g` only when the bonus wall-contact requirement is met and 15 kg is held; score `Load Scored / Structure Mass`. Cap Load Supported at 15,000 g. Include tiers and participation-only outcomes as statuses, not numeric estimates. Tiebreaks: structure mass, then estimated load closest to actual.

Any strength/failure simulator requires user-supplied assumptions and must be clearly labeled educational, not official scoring.
