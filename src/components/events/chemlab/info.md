# Chemistry Lab C — 2027 Rules Summary

**Status:** Verified against the supplied Division C Rules Manual, pp. C14–C15 (PDF pp. 19–20), checked October 8, 2026.

## Format and resources
- Team of up to 2; approximately 50 minutes; Eye Protection C; Class III calculator for each participant.
- Each participant may bring a writing utensil, one Class III calculator, one double-sided 8.5 × 11 in sheet (laminated/protected), and the equipment from the Division C Chemistry Events Lab Equipment List. Required lab equipment is not supplied. The ES supplies required reagents, extra glassware and needed references.
- Safety clothing: goggles, apron/lab coat, covered skin neck-to-wrist and toes, loose-fitting pants, tied-back shoulder-length-or-longer hair; violations/unsafe handling can be penalized through disqualification.

## Content
At least two activities are required, one each in **gases** and **kinetics**. Activities may be hands-on, questions, data analysis, or observation of an experiment; probe use/data interpretation may occur.
- **Gases:** physical properties and climate links (greenhouse gases/ozone depletion); Avogadro, Boyle, Charles, Dalton, Gay-Lussac, Graham and Ideal Gas laws; possible gas density, partial pressure, molar mass and diffusion tasks.
- **Kinetics:** measure rates and explain effects of temperature, concentration, particle size and catalysts. State/National also require differential/integrated rate laws from experiment/data, rate constants with units, and may include validating mechanisms and activation energy.
- Nomenclature, formula writing and stoichiometry (mole conversions, percent yield) are essential supporting tools and may be tested. Required ion knowledge expands at State/National; see 3.d for the exact lists.

## Scoring
High score wins; points are divided evenly between gases and kinetics. Task time is not a score or tiebreaker. Preselected questions break ties. Up to 10% cleanup penalty may apply.
