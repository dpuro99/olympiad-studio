# Designer Genes C — verified 2027 tool plan

Source: supplied 2027 Division C Rules Manual, pp. C20–C21. 2027 focus is genetics plus standard molecular biology (eukaryotic expression). No prior-year-only topics. Writing utensils, two Class II calculators, and one two-sided 8.5 × 11 in notes sheet are allowed; no clipboard-only restriction exists.

## Tool: Genetics Workbench
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Calculator
- **Goal:** Work through 2027 genetics problems that show work: Mendelian/classical genetics, molecular biology of a gene (DNA/RNA/protein), gene expression, crosses/pedigrees, population genetics, plus the manual's level-tagged topics.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Enter the given genotypes/allele data or a cross. 2) Generate/hold the Punnett-square, pedigree, or allele-frequency calculation with shown steps. 3) Review results against the manual §3 topic list (pp. C20–C21).
- **Content & data:** Manual §3 topics (Mendelian crosses incl. mono/di/trihybrid Punnett squares, pedigrees, allele frequencies and genetic drift, quantitative trait estimation, chromosomal gene mapping, molecular DNA/RNA/protein sequence logic); user-entered values only.
- **UI components:** Topic selector, input fields, step-by-step result panel.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Formulas and results shown as work; labeled practice, not official scoring.
- **Level gating:** core Regional; two-gene mapping and heritability at State; three-gene mapping, PCR/sequencing techniques, RNAi, CRISPR, ChIP-seq/Hi-C/RNA-seq at State and Nationals.
- **Persistence:** none.
- **Open questions:** Students may use scratch paper at some sites; decide whether work should be auto-saved.

## Tool: Eukaryotic Molecular Biology Practice
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Practice
- **Goal:** Practice standard-eukaryotic biology: DNA structure/replication, transcription, translation, protein structure, gene expression, and the listed advanced topics at higher levels.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Choose a topic and source-standard scenario. 2) Answer/classify the item (sequence logic, complement/antiparallel reads, amino-acid translation). 3) Check against manual-cited feedback.
- **Content & data:** Manual §3 topic list only (pp. C20–C21); no sequence/citation improvisation outside the manual's eukaryotic standard.
- **UI components:** Topic menu, prompt, answer validation, reference panel.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Feedback tied to manual objectives; practice only.
- **Level gating:** advanced topics level-gated per manual.
- **Persistence:** none.
- **Open questions:** Authoring content architecture needed if a larger bank is added.

This is a planning document only and does not invent official scoring.
