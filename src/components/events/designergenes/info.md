# Designer Genes C — 2027 Rules Summary

**Status:** Verified against the supplied Division C Rules Manual, pp. C20–C21 (PDF pp. 25–26), checked October 8, 2026.

## Format and resources
- Team of up to 2; approximately 50 minutes; Class II calculator.
- Each team may bring writing utensils, two Class II calculators and one double-sided 8.5 × 11 in notes sheet. Lamination/protection is allowed; multiple sheets and affixed labels are not.
- Written test, possibly arranged in stations. Topics are an exhaustive list and should not be overemphasized individually. Questions emphasize quantitative reasoning, calculations, experimental data, and evidence-based conclusions.

## 2027 topic scope
1. Mendelian genetics: inheritance laws and exceptions, mono-/di-/trihybrid crosses, probability, pedigrees, epistasis; State two-gene mapping, National three-gene mapping.
2. Mitosis/meiosis: stages, nondisjunction, karyotypes; State/National V(D)J recombination and class switching.
3. Population/evolutionary genetics: Hardy–Weinberg, allele-frequency mechanisms, additive traits, gene duplication/homologs/orthologs/paralogs, phylogenies; State/National broad-, narrow- and realized-heritability calculations.
4. DNA: structure/directionality, replication/fidelity, genome organization, damage/repair, mutations/protein consequences; State/National end-replication problem and telomeres.
5. **Eukaryotic** gene expression: central dogma, transcription, cis/trans regulation, yeast two-hybrid, RNA processing, translation; State/National mobile genetic elements. The old prokaryotic lac/trp operon content is not the listed 2027 scope.
6. Techniques: PCR, Illumina vs Nanopore, cloning (including Golden Gate), knockout/knockdown/complementation; State/National RNAi, homologous recombination/Cre-Lox, CRISPR, ChIP-seq, Hi-C and RNA-Seq.

Scoring is high score wins, with preselected tiebreak questions and credit for answer quality, reasoning and scientific method.
