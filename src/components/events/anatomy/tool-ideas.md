# Anatomy and Physiology C — verified 2027 tool plan

Source: supplied 2027 Division C Rules Manual, pp. C3–C5. Keep question banks segmented by Regional, State, and National scope; do not import prior-year nervous/senses/endocrine material.

## System-based station practice
Practice respiratory, digestive, and immune content using short-answer, image/model identification, quantitative questions, and data interpretation. Track the specific objective per question and distinguish core from State/National and National-only additions. Any disease or treatment content must come from the exact manual list.

## Respiratory data practice
Generate lung-volume/capacity, spirometry, flow-volume-loop, obstructive-vs-restrictive, gas exchange and oxygen-hemoglobin curve interpretation tasks from teacher-provided or cited datasets. Make higher-level regulation and extra disorders level-gated.

## Digestive/immune process practice
Use case-based prompts for digestion/absorption, motility, immunity, immunization, antibody classes and listed disorders. Higher-level content such as liver/pancreas pathways, V(D)J-related immunology/transplant concepts and National tumor immunology must be level-tagged.

This is a planning document only; it does not invent official scoring. Tie every item to the manual’s objective and cite the source provision in the dataset.
