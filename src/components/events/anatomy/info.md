# Anatomy and Physiology C — 2027 Rules Summary

**Status:** Verified against the supplied Division C Rules Manual, pp. C3–C5 (PDF pp. 8–10), checked October 8, 2026. This is a study summary, not a substitute for the manual.

## Format and permitted resources
- Team of up to 2; approximately 50 minutes; Class II calculator.
- Each team may bring writing utensils, two Class II calculators, and one 8.5 × 11 in sheet, both sides, any content/source. Lamination or a sheet protector is permitted; multiple sheets and affixed labels are prohibited.
- The event may be a written test or lab-practical stations; stations can use apparatus, models, illustrations, specimens, data, and problems.

## Current content: three systems
- **Respiratory:** anatomy and function; breathing mechanics and pressure; airway structures; lung volumes, spirometry and flow-volume loops; gas exchange and oxygen transport; exercise/altitude; specified disorders. State/National add respiratory regulation and additional disorders; National adds further disorders and prevention/diagnosis/treatment.
- **Digestive:** alimentary tract and accessory organs; abdominal wall and peritoneal relationships; canal wall layers; motility and hormonal/enteric regulation; mechanical/chemical digestion; nutrient absorption; exercise/obesity; specified disorders. State/National adds liver/pancreas cellular roles, metabolic/toxin effects, gut microbes and disorders; National adds further biliary/motility disorders, cancers and prevention/diagnosis/treatment.
- **Immune:** organs; innate and adaptive immunity; integration of immune responses; active/passive immunity and immunization; five immunoglobulin classes; specified immunodeficiencies, autoimmune diseases and hypersensitivities. State/National add signaling, detailed antibody structure, transplant rejection and additional disorders; National adds tumor immunology, vaccines/adjuvants, antibody-dependent enhancement and further disorders.

The specific required disease lists and level boundaries are detailed in the manual pp. C3–C5; use those lists rather than older A&P topics. Scoring is high score wins with preselected questions as tiebreakers.
