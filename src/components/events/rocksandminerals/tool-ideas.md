# Rocks and Minerals C — verified 2027 tool plan

Source: supplied 2027 Division C Rules Manual, pp. C52–C56 (main event C52–C54 plus the official 2027 rock/mineral list C55–C56). Scope is rocks, minerals, and rock-forming processes, balanced across igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic coverage. Identification is 30–50% of points; the rest is on topics and processes. Teams may bring the official specimen list, a binder of any size, and a field guide.

## Tool: Official-List Specimen Practice
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Practice
- **Goal:** Practice identifying 2027 official-list specimens across minerals, rocks, and rock-forming processes.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Choose a specimen from the official list or a random drill set. 2) Test yourself on key physical properties, classification, and year-specific emphasis. 3) Reveal the manual-backed answer and card details.
- **Content & data:** Official 2027 Division C specimen list (manual pp. C55–C56) grouped as mineral / rocks / rock-forming processes; hard-to-distinguish pairs flagged. No invented diagnostic criteria beyond manual/published sources.
- **UI components:** Specimen chooser, flashcard/prompt panel, reveal answer.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Practice-only; identification is 30–50% of event points, but this tool does not simulate official point breakdowns.
- **Level gating:** none (list is year-specific).
- **Persistence:** none.
- **Open questions:** Requires the official specimen list; confirm whether the full dataset will be supplied or entered via a management screen.

## Tool: Process and Diagram Practice
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Practice
- **Goal:** Practice rock-forming process identification and rock cycle / classification diagrams.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Pick a process (e.g., igneous/sedimentary/metamorphic formation conditions, rock cycle path). 2) Identify from a diagram or describe conditions. 3) Check against the manual's course content and specimen list.
- **Content & data:** Manual §3 topics, formation processes, and diagrams; no invented sample images.
- **UI components:** Diagram prompt, input, reference feedback.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Practice feedback only.
- **Level gating:** State/National thin-section photomicrograph skills gated.
- **Persistence:** none.
