# Rocks and Minerals C — verified 2027 tool plan

Source: supplied 2027 Division C Rules Manual, pp. C28–C31. This season is rocks, minerals, and rock-forming processes (metamorphic focus). Identifications are objective-driven, not score-weighted. A binder and a limited number of concise notes are permitted per the manual.

## Tool: Official-List Specimen Practice
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Practice
- **Goal:** Practice identifying 2027 official-list specimens across minerals, rocks, and rock-forming processes.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Choose a specimen from the official list or a random drill set. 2) Test yourself on key physical properties, classification, and year-specific emphasis. 3) Reveal the manual-backed answer and card details.
- **Content & data:** Official spec list (2027 Division C) grouped as mineral / rocks / rock-forming processes; hard-to-distinguish cards flagged. No invented diagnostic criteria beyond manual/published sources.
- **UI components:** Specimen chooser, flashcard/prompt panel, reveal answer.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Practice-only; identifies objective-driven, not score-weighted; no official score weights invented.
- **Level gating:** none (list is year-specific).
- **Persistence:** none.
- **Open questions:** Requires the official specimen list; confirm whether full dataset will be supplied or entered via management screen.

## Tool: Process and Diagram Practice
- **Status:** Planned
- **Category:** Practice
- **Goal:** Practice rock-forming process identification and rock cycle / classification diagrams from the manual.
- **Core interaction loop:** 1) Pick a process (e.g. metamorphic facies conditions, rock cycle path). 2) Identify from a diagram or describe conditions. 3) Check against manual-cited feedback.
- **Content & data:** Manual §3 show/display processes and diagrams (cited pages); no invented sample images.
- **UI components:** Diagram prompt, input, reference feedback.
- **Scoring & feedback:** Practice feedback only.
- **Level gating:** none.
- **Persistence:** none.
